Different Types of Betta Fish

Halfmoon Series

⌄
Includes: Halfmoon, Halfmoon Blue, Rosetail, Rosetail Blue
Halfmoon betta fish are the result of modern breeding, which became popular around the 1980s–1990s. They are among the most popular types and are often considered part of the premium category.
Characteristics: 180° tail spread, full and symmetrical fins.

Double Tail

⌄
Includes: Halfmoon, Halfmoon Blue, Rosetail, Rosetail Blue
Halfmoon betta fish are the result of modern breeding, which became popular around the 1980s–1990s. They are among the most popular types and are often considered part of the premium category.
Characteristics: 180° tail spread, full and symmetrical fins.

Plakat

⌄
Includes: Halfmoon, Halfmoon Blue, Rosetail, Rosetail Blue
Halfmoon betta fish are the result of modern breeding, which became popular around the 1980s–1990s. They are among the most popular types and are often considered part of the premium category.
Characteristics: 180° tail spread, full and symmetrical fins.

Crown Tail

⌄
Includes: Halfmoon, Halfmoon Blue, Rosetail, Rosetail Blue
Halfmoon betta fish are the result of modern breeding, which became popular around the 1980s–1990s. They are among the most popular types and are often considered part of the premium category.
Characteristics: 180° tail spread, full and symmetrical fins.

Feathertail

⌄
Includes: Feathertail
Feathertail betta fish are the result of modern crossbreeding developed since the early 2000s. They were bred to produce extremely ruffled fins that resemble feathers, making them more complex than Halfmoon varieties.
Characteristics: wavy, layered fins resembling feathers.

Dumbo

⌄
Includes: Elephant Ear, Dumbo, Dumbo Ear Butterfly
Dumbo betta fish are a variety developed through selective breeding to produce large pectoral fins resembling elephant ears. They became popular in the early 2000s and are known for their graceful and unique movement.
Characteristics: large, ear-like pectoral fins.

Giant Betta

⌄
Includes: Giant Betta
Giant betta fish are developed through selective breeding in Thailand in the early 2000s to produce a much larger body size than regular bettas. They are often used as a centerpiece due to their size.
Characteristics: significantly larger body size than regular bettas.

Koi Series

⌄
Includes: Yellow Koi, Nemo Emerald, Multy Cello / Cello Koi
Koi series betta fish are developed through selective crossbreeding to produce color patterns resembling Japanese koi fish. They became popular in the early 2000s, and each fish has a unique pattern.
Characteristics: mixed, random koi-like colors.

Dragon Scale

⌄
Includes: Dragon Scale
Dragon Scale betta fish are developed through selective crossbreeding to produce thick, iridescent scales resembling dragon armor. They became popular in the mid-2000s and are known for their bold, solid appearance.
Characteristics: thick, armor-like scales.

Fancy

⌄
Includes: FCCP Red Head, Mustas Glass
Fancy betta fish are developed through selective crossbreeding to produce diverse and irregular color combinations. They became popular in the modern era around the 2000s and are often the result of specialized breeding.
Characteristics: unique, non-standard color combinations.

Blue Rim

⌄
Includes: Blue Rim
Blue Rim betta fish are developed through selective crossbreeding to produce white fins with striking blue edges. They became popular in the modern era around the 2010s and are considered a sought-after variety.
Characteristics: contrasting fin edges (outline).
