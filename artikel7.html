Betta First Aid

By SiripJuara

January 26, 2023 | 21.03 WITA

First aid for betta fish is important when the fish shows signs of illness, stress, or injury. Some common signs include the fish appearing lethargic, less active, damaged fins, faded body color, or loss of appetite. These conditions are usually caused by poor water quality, sudden temperature changes, mild infections, or stress from environmental changes. If not treated promptly, the fish's condition can deteriorate and risk death. Therefore, first aid steps must be carried out quickly and correctly so the fish can recover.

The first step is to change the water with clean, conditioned water. Clean water helps reduce bacteria, waste, and harmful substances that can worsen the fish's condition. Water changes also help improve the quality of the environment so the fish can feel more comfortable during the recovery process.

If the fish shows signs of illness, it should be moved to a quarantine container. The quarantine process makes it easier to monitor the fish's condition and keeps water quality stable. A calmer quarantine environment also helps reduce stress on the fish, allowing recovery to take place more quickly.

In addition, adding a small amount of aquarium salt can help speed up the healing of minor wounds and reduce stress. Aquarium salt can also help inhibit the growth of mild bacteria and fungi. However, salt must be used carefully and not excessively to avoid disrupting the fish's condition. Use 1 teaspoon per 10 liters — never use table salt or iodized salt.

During the recovery process, it is important to keep the environment calm and the water temperature stable. Avoid moving the fish too frequently or placing the container in a busy area as this can add to the fish's stress. Additionally, feeding can be temporarily reduced to maintain water quality and allow the fish to focus on recovery.
