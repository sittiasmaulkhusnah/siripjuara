History of Betta Fish

The name "Betta" comes from an ancient warrior clan, reflecting the fish's aggressive nature.

In Thailand during the 19th century, they were known as "Siamese Fighting Fish" and highly popular.

In the late 1800s, betta fish were finally introduced to the Western world.

Different Types of Betta Fish

Halfmoon Series ▼

Includes: Halfmoon, Halfmoon Blue, Rosetail, Rosetail Blue

Halfmoon betta fish are the result of modern breeding. They are among the most popular types and are often considered part of the premium category.

Characteristics: 180° tail spread, full and symmetrical fins.

Double Tail ▼

Includes: Includes: Halfmoon, Halfmoon Blue, Rosetail, Rosetail Blue

Halfmoon betta fish are the result of modern breeding, which became popular around the 1980s–1990s. They are among the most popular types and are often considered part of the premium category.

Characteristics: 180° tail spread, full and symmetrical fins.
